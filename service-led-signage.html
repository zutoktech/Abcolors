LED sign board FAQs

Quick answers to the questions customers ask us most. Need something else? Call or WhatsApp +91 98422 75299.

What types of LED sign boards do you make?

We make 3D acrylic LED letters, stainless steel (SS 304) lip letters, titanium gold letters, glow sign boards, router-cut ACP cladding boards and totem pylon signs for shops, showrooms, offices and commercial buildings.

Are your LED signs suitable for outdoor use?

Yes. Our illuminated signs use waterproof IP67-rated LED modules that give bright, even light with low power consumption and a long working life, so they suit outdoor shop fronts and building facades.

Do you install the sign board as well?

Yes. We handle the complete job: design, fabrication in our own workshop and on-site installation.

What details should I share for an LED sign board quote?

Share the board size (for example 10 x 4 ft), the letter type you prefer (3D acrylic, stainless steel or ACP), whether it should be illuminated and the installation location. Photos of the site help us suggest the best option.
