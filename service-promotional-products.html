Promotional products FAQs

Quick answers to the questions customers ask us most. Need something else? Call or WhatsApp +91 98422 75299.

Which promotional display products do you supply?

We supply roll-up standees, sandwich acrylic boards, branded event canopies, teardrop and feather flags, table flags, fabric light boxes and promotional banners for expos, store launches, exhibitions and events.

How long does a roll-up standee take to set up?

Our roll-up standees use lightweight, heavy-duty aluminium hardware and can be set up in about two minutes without any tools.

What print quality do you use for standees and banners?

Graphics are printed at 1440 DPI, which keeps text sharp and colours rich even when viewed up close.

Do you offer bulk rates for campaigns?

Yes, we offer bulk corporate and campaign rates. Share the quantity and sizes you need in the enquiry form and we will send a quotation.
