Corporate gifting FAQs

Quick answers to the questions customers ask us most. Need something else? Call or WhatsApp +91 98422 75299.

What corporate gifts can you customise?

We customise employee joining kits, executive diaries, laser-engraved pens and card holders, drinkware, acrylic and wooden trophies, mementos, lanyards and gift hampers with your brand.

Can you handle both small and large orders?

Yes. We handle small runs as well as large volumes, for example 50 award pieces or 5,000 customised joining hampers. Share your quantity in the enquiry form for a quotation.

How are the gifts branded?

Branding is done in-house using UV printing, laser engraving and foil stamping for a clean, lasting finish.

What is the Touch Art Frame?

The Touch Art Frame is our newly launched premium gift: an elegant, fully customisable frame for weddings, anniversaries, corporate events and other special occasions.
