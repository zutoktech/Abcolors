ABOUT AB COLORS /
SIGNAGE, BRANDING & PRINTING IN TRICHY

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions customers in Trichy and across Tamil Nadu ask us most often.

What does AB Colors do?

AB Colors is a signage, branding and printing company in Tiruchirappalli (Trichy), Tamil Nadu. We design, manufacture and install LED sign boards and 3D letter signs, ACP cladding, glow sign boards, promotional displays such as roll-up standees and canopies, shop and office branding, flatbed direct UV printing, corporate gifts and digital printing such as visiting cards, brochures and flyers.

Where is AB Colors located in Trichy?

Our office is on the 4th floor of Pearls Tower, 26 Karur Bypass Road, opposite Kalaignar Arivalayam and next to Taj Marriage Hall, Kela Chinthamani, Tiruchirappalli 620002. Our manufacturing units are in Sanjeevi Nagar, Trichy.

How much does a sign board cost?

The price depends on the board size, the material (flex, acrylic, ACP, stainless steel or titanium letters), whether it is lit with LEDs and where it will be installed. Share your size and requirement through the quote form or on WhatsApp at +91 98422 75299 and we will send you a free quotation.

Do you work with businesses outside Trichy?

Yes. We are based in Trichy and deliver signage, branding and printing projects for businesses across Tamil Nadu. Mention your city in the enquiry form and we will confirm delivery and installation for your location.

Do you make Tamil and English sign boards?

Yes. We regularly produce Tamil, English and bilingual boards, from acrylic and LED shop signs to name boards and printed displays, and our design team can prepare the artwork for you.

How quickly will you respond to my enquiry?

Our team usually calls or WhatsApps you within 2 to 4 working hours of receiving your enquiry. The office is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:30 PM.
