Flatbed UV printing FAQs

Quick answers to the questions customers ask us most. Need something else? Call or WhatsApp +91 98422 75299.

What is flatbed direct UV printing?

Flatbed UV printing prints your design directly onto a rigid surface using UV-cured inks, so there is no vinyl sticker to peel or bubble. The ink cures instantly under UV light, giving sharp and durable prints.

Which materials can you UV print on?

We print on acrylic, toughened glass, wood, MDF, ACP, foam board, sun board, gypsum board and metal, with material thickness up to 100 mm (4 inches).

Can UV prints include white ink and raised effects?

Yes. We print CMYK with high-density white ink and spot gloss varnish, which allows tactile, 3D embossed effects.

Are UV prints suitable for outdoor use?

Yes. UV prints resist sunlight, moisture and scratches, so they work for indoor decor as well as outdoor signage.
