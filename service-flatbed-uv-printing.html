Flatbed Direct UV Printing

AB Colors provides advanced Flatbed Direct UV Printing Services using state-of-the-art multi-head UV LED flatbed printers. We print directly onto acrylic, toughened glass, wood, MDF, ACP, foam board, and metal without needing vinyl pasting.

Featuring CMYK + High-Density White ink and Spot Gloss Varnish capabilities, our UV prints achieve incredible detail, tactile 3D embossed effects, and outstanding resistance to sunlight, moisture, and scratching.

  • Direct Print on Acrylic, Glass, Wood & ACP
  • High-Density White Ink & Spot Varnish
  • Scratch, Water & UV Sunlight Resistant
  • Substrates up to 100mm (4 inches) Thick
