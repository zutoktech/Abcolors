SIGN BOARD MAKER /
in Trichy

For nearly two decades, AB Colors has been delivering innovative Signage, Branding, and Printing Solutions across Tamil Nadu. As a complete printing and signage company, we specialize in Custom Sign Boards, Large-Format Digital Printing, Flatbed Printing, Vehicle Branding, and premium printing solutions on materials such as Glass, Acrylic, and Foam Boards.

Our experienced creative team offers professional Graphic Design Services, including Logo Design, Business Cards, Brochures, Flyers, Promotional Materials, Vehicle Graphics, and Corporate Branding Solutions tailored to businesses of all sizes. We also provide Customized Wallpaper Printing and a wide range of Promotional Products for commercial, retail, and home branding requirements.

We are proud to introduce our newly launched Touch Art Frame — a premium and elegant personalized gifting solution designed for Weddings, Anniversaries, Corporate Events, and Special Occasions, fully customized to match your unique requirements.

About AB Colors: quick facts

Common questions about who we are and how we work.

Who founded AB Colors?

AB Colors was founded and is run by Arun Kumar and Balaji, who lead the company together as a team.

How long has AB Colors been in business?

AB Colors has been creating signage, branding and printing solutions for businesses across Tamil Nadu for nearly two decades.

Does AB Colors have its own manufacturing facility?

Yes. Signs and prints are produced in our own units in Sanjeevi Nagar, Trichy: Unit 1 at No. 3, Makilampoo Street and Unit 2 at 10, Nehru Street.
